Welcome to the Series

Welcome to
The Golden Table

Welcome, and thank you for joining us! We are delighted to welcome you to the Executive Chef Leadership Series: The Golden Table, an exclusive professional development experience created for Executive Chefs across The Golden State Chapter of CMAA.

Representing more than 300 private clubs, our Chapter is home to some of the finest culinary professionals in the industry. Yet, to date, fewer than 10% of our clubs are participating in this unique leadership series.

The Golden Table was designed to bring Executive Chefs together to exchange ideas, strengthen leadership skills, explore innovative culinary techniques, and build meaningful professional relationships within the private club community.

We are excited to have you as part of this exceptional journey.

Jack Dykstra

Executive Chef

The Los Angeles Country Club

Chef Jack Dykstra is committed to delivering exceptional culinary experiences through personalized service, mentorship, and leadership. With nearly two decades of experience, he is passionate about developing future culinary professionals while creating memorable dining experiences for members and guests.

Thayer Johnson

Executive Chef

Orinda Country Club

Chef Thayer Johnson brings more than thirty years of culinary experience to the Executive Chef Leadership Series. Known for his innovative menus, technical expertise, and passion for hospitality, he continues to inspire excellence within the private club industry.

Colin Moody, CEC, PCII

Director of Culinary Operations

Sherwood Country Club

Chef Colin Moody leads the culinary program at Sherwood Country Club with a progressive approach centered on innovation, sustainability, and locally sourced ingredients. His extensive private club experience and commitment to mentoring culinary professionals have earned him recognition throughout the industry.
